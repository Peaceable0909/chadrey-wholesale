How it works

Wholesale from Thai dealers, priced fairly as the exchange rate moves.

1

Browse

Pick products and see today's price. The more you order, the lower the unit price.

2

Request a quote

Choose quantity and options and send your details. It's free and you pay nothing yet.

3

Get your quotation

We confirm the current price and stock with the dealer and send an estimate you can accept.

4

Pay the invoice

After you accept, you get an invoice with the final product cost, shipping and duties.

5

We ship

Your goods leave Thailand and the delivery window is on your invoice.

Why aren't prices fixed?

Goods are bought in Thai baht and the exchange rate, dealer prices and freight change. Showing today's price and confirming it in a quotation means we never promise a number we can't keep.
