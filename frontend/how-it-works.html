Why we quote instead of listing prices

Three documents, not one price tag

We separate your order into three stages — a Quote Request, a Quotation, and a final Invoice — because a single listed price can't account for exchange rates, supplier cost, quantity, or shipping until we actually know your order.

01

Quote Request

You pick a product, quantity, and preferences, and send us your details. No price is generated yet — this is just your requirements reaching us.

02

We review

We check real supplier cost, quantity-based pricing, shipping options, and any duties for your destination.

03

Quotation

You get a preliminary price — an estimate, clearly marked as subject to change, so we're never promising a number we can't keep.

04

Final Invoice

Once costs are locked in, you get a proper invoice: product cost, shipping, tax/duties, and any other charges, plus a dispatch and delivery estimate.

What protects you (and us)

Because pricing is confirmed in two steps rather than one, we're never locked into a number before we've actually purchased from the supplier — if the exchange rate or supplier price shifts between your quote and your invoice, that's reflected honestly rather than absorbed silently or passed on without explanation.
